Falling Kitten support

Help with the Falling Kitten Android game.

Contact

Email lukas.petersson.1999@gmail.com. Support is provided by Lukas Petersson, LP Apps, in Sweden.

What to include

Tell us the app version, Android version, device model, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help if it does not contain information you prefer not to share.

Keep personal information private. Do not email passwords, authentication codes, payment details, identity documents, precise location, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Players under 18 should ask a parent or guardian for help before contacting us.

High scores

High scores stay in the app’s local storage. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes them, subject to how your device handles deletion. The game has no online leaderboard or account recovery, so LP Apps cannot restore a lost score.

Privacy questions

Use the same address and identify Falling Kitten in your message. See the privacy notice for details about the app and voluntary support correspondence.
