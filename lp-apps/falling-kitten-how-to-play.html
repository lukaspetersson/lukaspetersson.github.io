How to play Falling Kitten

Turn falling kittens so they land upright. This guide covers Falling Kitten 1.2 for Android.

Your first round

  1. Start a game. The score begins at 0 and you have three hearts.
  2. Tap a falling kitten to turn it. Each tap turns that kitten by a quarter turn.
  3. A kitten that lands upright adds one point. A kitten that does not land upright costs one heart.
  4. Keep turning kittens as they fall. When all three hearts are gone, the round ends.

Does it get harder? As a round continues, kittens fall faster and arrive more often. The challenge is keeping them upright as the pace picks up.

Practice a quarter turn

Tap the kitten until its paws point down. This is a simple browser illustration, not the Android game.

In Falling Kitten for Android, you turn kittens while they fall. This illustration stays still so you can learn the rotation at your own pace.

During and after a round

The pause menu offers resume, restart and main-menu options. After a round ends, the game shows your score and the local high score. Tap the replay icon to start another round.
