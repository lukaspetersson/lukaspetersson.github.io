How to play Falling Kitten

Turn falling kittens so they land upright. This guide covers Falling Kitten 1.2 for Android.

Your first round

  1. Start a game. The score begins at 0 and you have three hearts.
  2. Tap a falling kitten to turn it. Each tap turns that kitten by a quarter turn.
  3. A kitten that lands upright adds one point. A kitten that does not land upright costs one heart.
  4. Keep turning kittens as they fall. When all three hearts are gone, the round ends.

During and after a round

Use the pause button to stop the action. From the pause screen, you can resume, restart the round, or return to the main menu. After a round ends, the game shows your score and the local high score. Tap the replay icon to start another round.
